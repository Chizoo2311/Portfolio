03 · Featured Project

Finstock — Inventory & Sales Management System

My main showcase piece: a full-stack MERN application built for a real hardware and home-finishing business.

Finstock

Full-Stack Developer · 2026

Finstock replaces manual, spreadsheet-based stock tracking with one system that covers the full retail loop — from receiving supplier stock to ringing up a sale at the counter. I handled both the frontend and backend: database schema, REST API, and the React interface staff use every day.

MongoDBExpress.jsReactNode.jsMERN Stack
  • Product catalog, stock management, and inventory status tracking with low-stock alerts
  • Supplier & procurement workflow, from purchase orders to receiving
  • Point-of-sale flow with live cart, VAT calculation, and checkout
  • Sales reports and an AI-assisted demand insights panel for restocking decisions
  • Transaction history covering POS sales, purchase orders, and item replacements
  • Role-based login for staff, with a dashboard summarizing daily activity
